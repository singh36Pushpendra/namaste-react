What is the importance of test cases?
How many types of testing.

Developer testing

Developer can do two types of testing:
1) Manual testing
2) Writing test cases.

1) Unit Testing -> testing one unit or one component in isolation.

2) Integration Testing -> testing integration of the components.

3) End to End Testing -> Simulating what user do throughout the app.
                        (requires different types of tools)

As a Developer we are majorly concern about the first two testing.
(Unit, Integration).
